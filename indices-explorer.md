## Concept landscape

Each cell counts published methodology leaves—indicators, questions,
criteria, or model fields—tagged to a common concept. The tags aid
comparison; they do not replace the publishers’ own structures.

Select a row or cell to compare. Counts reflect the most detailed
disclosed framework level, not equal units of methodological complexity;
separately scoped analytical and raw-input totals remain in each
profile.

## Native methodologies

Publisher structures remain intact—from simple ratios to asymmetric
hierarchies, lattices, model inputs, and assessment criteria.

Expand branches to inspect every documented leaf. Shared concept tags
appear as context, never as claims that two publisher indicators are
identical.
